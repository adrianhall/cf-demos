## Themes and observations

- **Accessibility is the main pre-release work.** All P1s and 9 of the 34 findings share one root
  cause: focus and status are not managed after a dialog closes or a mutation succeeds. A shared
  return-focus helper and a shared live-region announcer, applied across dialogs, boards, and stream
  rows, fix them together.
- **The security posture is strong.** RS256 is pinned and audience/expiry are enforced. The
  dev-token bypass compiles to `false` in production. Every mutation goes through same-origin and
  JSON-only checks, Markdown is sanitized in two layers, all SQL goes through one bound-parameter
  helper, and the Data Worker has no workers.dev URL, preview, or route. What remains is headers,
  dev-only CVEs, and hardening.
- **The architecture is sound.** The four tiers are real and imports only point downward (verified
  by grep). Composition happens once per invocation, handlers never construct repositories, and the
  RPC contract uses an `RpcResult` envelope with `#`-private internals. The D1 “read, then one
  atomic batch” port with `tx_guard` is the demo's best teaching pattern.
